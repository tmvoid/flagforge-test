ONE COMMAND

Your lab starts in one command.

The launcher downloads the mission bundle, verifies its checksum, checks it against a safety policy, and brings the whole environment up on your own machine. No signup, no queue, no shared infrastructure.

  • Runs on 127.0.0.1 — nothing is exposed to the internet
  • Every image and port is verified before it starts
  • Labs stop themselves after their time limit

HOW IT WORKS

Three pieces, one click.

The site talks to a browser extension, the extension talks to a native app, and the app runs the lab. Each link is locked down, so you are never handing a stranger the keys to your machine.
